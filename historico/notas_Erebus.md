# Erebus (476) – notas
Grande: ~130 relógios no site (Ascent 34/36/39/41, HAQ 36/39/41, Ascent 40 GMT, Titan, Origin 40/GMT/41, Nexus, Twenty-Four 38/40, Mother of Pearl). Fundada 2022 (WatchPatrol). Logo: https://erebuswatches.com/cdn/shop/files/erebus-logo-black.svg?v=1742277078
Não usa códigos (só nome+cor). Coleções do menu: polar-cola, ascent-haq-burgundy-gilt, bronze-aventurine, nexus-premium, origin-limted-edition, the-new-black-ascent-41, erebus-origin, erebus-titan...
Fundadores: Jody Musgrove (canal "Just One More Watch", escocês na Austrália) e Steven Parker; fundada 2022.
Origin (1º modelo, 41 mm, NH35A, 200 m): 8 cores black, blue, white, orange, purple, teal, red, yellow. Depois Origin 38 (38,5 mm), Origin 40 (gen 2), Origin GMT (NH34).
Ascent original (39 mm, Miyota 9015) com mostradores minerais Malachite, Aventurine, Tigers Eye.
Pendentes: Ascent Malachite (39) e Origin Black (41) — fotos; Origin 41 demais cores; Origin 38 Blue/Black.
